Magnesium Malate: A Mineral With Many Jobs

Magnesium is an essential mineral involved in hundreds of enzyme systems. It contributes to normal muscle and nerve function, protein synthesis, blood glucose regulation, and energy metabolism. ATP commonly functions in a complex with magnesium, so magnesium status is relevant to many reactions that use cellular energy.

The manufacturer lists magnesium malate in Advanced Mitochondrial Formula. Malate refers to a form in which magnesium is paired with malic acid. The label should be checked to see how much elemental magnesium is supplied per serving. The weight of a magnesium compound is not the same as the amount of magnesium itself.

Getting enough magnesium from food is important. Sources include leafy greens, nuts, seeds, beans, and whole grains. A supplement may be useful when intake is inadequate or a clinician identifies a need, but taking more than necessary does not guarantee more energy. Magnesium status and appropriate intake are individual matters.

High amounts of supplemental magnesium can cause diarrhea, nausea, and abdominal cramping. Magnesium can also interfere with absorption of certain antibiotics and oral bisphosphonates if taken too close together. People with kidney problems or those using medications that affect magnesium should review the label with a healthcare professional.
